## **7\. FAQ**

# **A few answers before you book a demo.**

### **Is Steps AI only for online stores?**

No. You can use it for enquiries, appointments, lead capture and customer support across different businesses. Store integrations add product, checkout and order features for ecommerce.

### **Can it do more than reply to messages?**

Yes. It can collect lead details, book appointments and create tickets. The platform also lets you run campaigns and build workflows for follow-up and other tasks.

### **How does it know about my business?**

You add your website, FAQs and documents, then connect the tools it needs. The agent uses your information and instructions when it replies.

### **Can my team take over?**

Yes. Your team can take over with the conversation history available. You can also set rules for when the agent should ask for human help.

### **Do all features work on every channel?**

No. WhatsApp supports broadcasts, Shopify cart recovery and COD confirmation. Instagram supports comment-to-DM and story triggers. Your demo will show what works on the channels you use.

### **Do I need a developer?**

You can set up your agent and build workflows without coding. The setup depends on the jobs, channels and tools you want to connect.

# **Customer evidence: placement and use**

Use the supplied testimonials as customer evidence. Compact cards use exact excerpts, with the full original quote available in an expanded view. Place relevant excerpts beside demonstrations, and four different stories in the main testimonial section. Customer logos can sit under the hero when the actual assets are available. Testimonials do not establish measured case studies or results for other capabilities.

* Preserve the exact wording, spelling and attribution of quoted passages. The site’s own copy uses Steps AI; customer quotes retain StepsAI where supplied.  
* A full case study requires a documented customer situation, implementation, result and timeframe. Link the customer-story CTA to /case-studies/, but do not label these quotes as measured case studies or invent individual story URLs.  
* The supplied proof covers product recommendations, lead capture, sales preparation, repeated questions, after-hours support, Instagram DMs and help-centre answers. It does not directly prove broadcast performance, cart recovery, COD confirmation, booking lift or marketing revenue. Use product demonstrations for those capabilities until customer evidence is available.
